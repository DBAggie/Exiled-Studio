# Community builds: sources and credit

These builds are credited to their creators and included as test cases. If you created one of them and want it removed from this repository, open an issue at https://github.com/DBAggie/Exiled-Studio/issues (or contact the maintainers) and we will remove it.

Removing a build also removes its entry in `../fixtures.json` and any divergence-register entry or test that names it (ADR-007 Amendment 1).

## What these files are
Path of Building (PoB) build files published by the creators below, used only as test fixtures for the PoB oracle (`tools/oracle/`, Spike C) and later for the successor's calculations. They are not shown, sold, or distributed as content in the application. The basis for keeping them in this repository is ADR-007 Amendment 1 (`docs/adr/007-corpus-and-coverage.md`).

Each file is the build's PoB export code, decoded (URL-safe base64, then zlib) to XML. The only change: the attributes `lastAccountHash` and `lastCharacterHash` (account identifiers) are removed where present; nothing else is changed. "Downloaded SHA-256" is the decoded export; "SHA-256" is the committed file, also pinned in `../fixtures.json`. All were retrieved on 2026-11-10.

## Terms of the sources (as found on 2026-11-10)
- **Maxroll** (maxroll.gg): the guides link to the Ziff Davis terms of use (https://www.ziffdavis.com/terms-of-use). They allow downloading and copying content for personal, non-commercial use, and otherwise forbid modifying, publishing, reproducing, or distributing the content without permission, and retrieving it with automated tools. Keeping these builds here does not fit those terms; the maintainer accepted that for test use on 2026-11-10 (ADR-007 Amendment 1), with credit and removal on request. No consent has been asked for or given: on 2026-11-10 the maintainer decided not to contact the creators for now and to remove any build on request instead.
- **pohx.net**: the site says it "was created and is maintained by Jadeon77 along with contributions from the PoE and Pohx communities" and is "free to use". It states no terms of use and no license. The PoB codes are hosted on pobb.in, which states no terms either.
- **Video guides** (second batch): creators publish PoB codes on pobb.in next to their YouTube guides; poebuilds.cc (a fan-made index of guides by views, https://www.poebuilds.cc/) was used to find them and their view counts. No license is stated; the builds are the creators', credited below with the guide.

The builds were fetched with a few manual requests: Maxroll's guide pages and its guide index (https://maxroll.gg/poe/build-guides), its PoB links (`https://maxroll.gg/poe/pob/<id>`, served from `https://planners.maxroll.gg/profiles/load/poe/<id>`), pobb.in's raw codes (`https://pobb.in/<id>/raw`), and four poebuilds.cc list pages (mine, Gladiator, minion, totem, Occultist builds, by views). No fetching is part of the project's code or CI.

## Agreed but not included, and the substitutes
These guides on the agreed list publish no PoB export (their pages embed only a Maxroll planner) as of 2026-11-10: Poison SRS Necromancer, Earthshatter Slayer, Lightning Arrow Deadeye, Explosive Arrow Ballista Champion, Ice Trap Trickster, Rolling Magma Mines Saboteur, Eviscerate Bleed Gladiator. Substitutes were chosen so each mechanic is covered by a build its creator published for 3.29 (or 3.28) whose active setup is an endgame one, preferring builds with many views: traps (Lightning Trap of Sparking), totems (Holy Flame Totem; Kinetic Fusillade ballistae), mines (Ethereal Knives mines), bleed with block (Lacerate Gladiator), Necromancer minions (Forged Frostbearer spectres), Summon Raging Spirit and Animate Guardian (the Guardian build), and three more widely played archetypes (Flicker Strike, Archmage Shock Nova, Essence Drain Contagion). Earthshatter is covered by the Facebreaker Luminary build, bows by Elemental Hit of the Spectrum.

Considered and left out: Kinetic Fusillade Ballista from its Maxroll guide (`djn1f60t`; opens on a level 13 levelling setup, so the endgame code from the creator's video is used), Bleed Gladiator from another video (`1ohmrWF6fKnQ`; an Act 1 setup), Winter Orb Occultist (`maxroll.gg/poe/pob/5yi5t30x`; a level 31 setup; Winter Orb is covered), the Luminary mercenary build (`poe.ninja/poe1/pob/9360e`; its main skill is Frostblink, so it would test little), Storm Burst of Repulsion Totem and Carrion Golems (the same mechanics as builds already in).
